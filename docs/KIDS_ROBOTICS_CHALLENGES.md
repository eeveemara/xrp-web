# XRP Kids Robotics Challenges

These challenges are designed for students who have little or no programming experience.

## Challenge 1 — Move the robot

Make the XRP drive forward 12 inches.

Then try 24 inches.

**Goal:** Learn that the number on the block changes how far the robot travels.

## Challenge 2 — Go and come back

Make the robot:

1. Drive forward 12 inches.
2. Drive backward 12 inches.

**Goal:** Learn that blocks run from top to bottom.

## Challenge 3 — Make a turn

Make the robot:

1. Drive forward 12 inches.
2. Turn right 90 degrees.
3. Drive forward 12 inches.

Try changing the angle to 45 degrees and 180 degrees.

## Challenge 4 — Make a square

Use a `repeat 4 times` block.

Inside it, put:

- Drive forward 18 inches
- Turn right 90 degrees

**Goal:** Introduce loops.

## Challenge 5 — Navigate the course

Give the student a taped course with a start area and finish area.

They must choose the distances and turns needed to reach the finish.

## Instructor tips

- Encourage students to predict what will happen before pressing Run.
- If the robot misses the target, ask what number should change.
- Do not immediately give the answer.
- Let students discover that changing a parameter changes the robot's behavior.
- Have an instructor hold the robot or be ready to stop power during first tests.
